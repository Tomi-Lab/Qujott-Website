Privacy Policy

1. Qujott Application

Qujott stores all your notes locally on your device or in the storage folder you designate.
• No user accounts or login required
• No proprietary cloud servers
• No in-app analytics or behavioral tracking

2. Website Analytics & Cookies

Our website (qujott.com) uses privacy-respecting analytics tools, including Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, to analyze aggregated traffic patterns, understand how visitors interact with our pages, and improve website performance. These services collect anonymous metrics (such as page views, device types, and referral sources) and do not access or collect any note contents or personal data.

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at support@qujott.com.

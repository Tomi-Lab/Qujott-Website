Qujott 1.3.0
November 2026

✨ New Features & Improvements

  • In-Note Search:
    Quickly search within the open note using Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on macOS) with instant match highlighting and navigation.
  • Mermaid Diagram Rendering:
    Render flowcharts, sequence diagrams, class diagrams, ER diagrams, Gantt charts, and mind maps directly inside Markdown notes.
  • Mermaid Quick Insert:
    Effortlessly insert a blank Mermaid block or flowchart template from the formatting menu without remembering syntax.
  • Expanded 12 Languages:
    Qujott now officially supports 12 languages across the entire application.
Qujott 1.2.0
November 2026

✨ New Features & Improvements

  • UI Display Scaling Support (Windows):
    Added UI scaling for PC displays. Adjust the overall interface size (80%, 100%, 120%, 140%, 160%) to suit your screen, improving readability and accessibility across menus, toolbars, and note lists.

    Seamlessly combines with editor text sizing (A-- to A++) for a more comfortable writing experience on high-resolution monitors.

    Eliminated edge margin gaps when zoomed out to 80% and refined button visibility and spacing at higher scale factors.
